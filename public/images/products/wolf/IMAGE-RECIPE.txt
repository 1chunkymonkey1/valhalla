Fenrir product-in-environment recipe
====================================

Goal: adventure motorcycle composited into Eason’s real Wolf / launch plates
(snow whiteout, wolf-ref winter slope, Luke frost meadow / mountain bridge).

Ranked tools for THIS workflow (plate + vehicle insert):
  1. Gemini image edit (Nano Banana / Gemini 2.5 Flash Image) — best plate lock:
     upload the real photo, instruct “add motorcycle, keep background pixels”
  2. GPT-image (ChatGPT image edit) — strong edit + lighting match on uploaded plate
  3. Flux (Kontext / Fill / Redux) — excellent photoreal; use img2img or inpaint
     with the plate as control; great when you already have a cutout bike
  4. Midjourney — gorgeous bikes, weaker plate fidelity unless --cref/--sref /
     remix on the exact plate; easy to drift from the real location

In-repo now: GenerateImage seed assets using public/images/wolf.png,
sites/wolf-ref.jpg, and plates in ../_plates/. Replace with Gemini/GPT edits
on those plates for final production stills.

Prompt skeleton (paste with plate attached):
  Photoreal electric adventure motorcycle (matte charcoal / dark iron, knobby
  tires, no logos) standing in THIS exact photograph. Preserve the original
  landscape, trees, snow texture, and camera grain. Match overcast light and
  cast a soft shadow consistent with the plate. Tires slightly sunk in snow.
  No rider, no UI, no purple grade, no CGI glow. Documentary product photo.
